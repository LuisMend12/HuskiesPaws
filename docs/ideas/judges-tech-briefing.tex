\ideatitle{HuskiesPaws}{How to present the stack: Phone $\rightarrow$ API $\rightarrow$ World.}

Lead with the product, then name three layers. Judges remember a loop better than a
laundry list of libraries.

\section{What to say in one breath}
HuskiesPaws is a \textbf{phone app}. Shared game rules live in plain JavaScript.
A small Node API holds secrets and talks to Grok. Place facts come from Wikipedia;
walks come from real routing. If Grok is out of credits, the app still plays.

That is the architecture. Everything else is detail.

\section{The stack, in the order you should tell it}

\subsection{The thing they hold --- Expo / React Native (SDK 57)}
Expo Go on iPhone. Map, GPS, camera, step counter, on-device speech. Demo Walk for
the indoor table. If they ask ``native or web?'': \textbf{native phone app}. The web
prototype was dropped.

\subsection{The rules --- \texttt{core/} (plain JS)}
Pets, eggs, ranks, turf, ISS rarity, geo. Same files for the phone and iMessage.
Not an LLM deciding the game.

\subsection{The world --- tools, not vibes}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Wikipedia} for nearby places and the postcard fact.
  \item \textbf{OpenStreetMap / OSRM} for the walking path.
  \item \textbf{wheretheiss.at} for ``ISS overhead $\rightarrow$ rarer hatch.''
\end{itemize}
Agents only speak what those tools return.

\subsection{The API --- Node (\texttt{server/})}
No extra runtime dependencies. Keys stay here: \texttt{XAI\_API\_KEY},
\texttt{ELEVENLABS\_API\_KEY}. Rate limits and cached Imagine images. The phone never
embeds keys.

\subsection{Voice and pictures (optional, credit-dependent)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Grok Voice} for Pip and Fern when the key and credits work.
  \item \textbf{ElevenLabs} for Moss's stories.
  \item \textbf{Grok Imagine} for postcards and hatch art.
  \item Fallback: \textbf{expo-speech} plus original SVG art.
\end{itemize}
Say the fallback \emph{before} they hear on-device TTS.

\subsection{Extra, only if they ask}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Photon Spectrum} --- same squad over iMessage.
  \item \textbf{MapLibre / OpenFreeMap} --- garden 3D map, \textbf{iOS development
        build only}; Expo Go is Apple Maps.
  \item \textbf{Supabase / Render} --- written; do not claim live unless
        \texttt{/api/health} is green.
  \item \textbf{Cursor} --- SpaceX track: built in Agent mode, logged in
        \texttt{CURSOR\_LOG.md}.
\end{itemize}

Do \textbf{not} pitch Nessie, background tracking, or a live cloud host unless you
have just checked health.

\section{How to present it at the table (about 2 minutes)}
Keep the pitch deck on \textbf{slide 4} (Demo Walk on). Hand them the phone.

\begin{tabular}{@{}P{0.12\textwidth}P{0.28\textwidth}P{0.54\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Time} & \textbf{You do} & \textbf{You say (tech, lightly)} \\
\midrule
0:00 & Title / map & Everyday walks, creature adventure.
  \textbf{Navigation} $=$ curiosity, not shortest path. \\
0:15 & Squad $\rightarrow$ Pip $\rightarrow$ Explore & Pip is a \textbf{Scout role}.
  Wikipedia finds a real place. \\
0:35 & Postcard $\rightarrow$ Start the walk & Fern \textbf{routes} you. Flowers are
  the trail. Demo Walk is simulated. \\
1:05 & Capture / egg & Walking earns the collectible. ISS can make hatches rarer. \\
1:25 & One sentence of architecture & Phone $\rightarrow$ our API $\rightarrow$
  Wikipedia, maps, optional Grok. Secrets not in the app. \\
1:40 & Cursor & Built in \textbf{Cursor}; Grok Imagine/Voice when credits allow. \\
1:50 & Close & Hand them the phone. ``Ask us anything.'' \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

If Grok is slow or silent: ``That's the fallback. Game still runs.'' Then keep moving.

\section{Answers they will actually ask}

\paragraph{Does the AI make up buildings?}
No. Scout and story only use Wikipedia. The postcard can show the source link.

\paragraph{Where is the model?}
Not in the phone. Optional Grok on the server. Game rules are ordinary code.

\paragraph{Why Expo?}
iPhone demo this weekend without a full App Store build. Camera, location, and
speech are Expo modules.

\paragraph{Is it deployed?}
\texttt{render.yaml} is ready. Default is local file storage. Do not say ``it's live
on Render'' unless health is true.

\paragraph{What did Cursor do?}
Walk-loop UX, art, Cloudflare tunnel for guest Wi-Fi, deck from real captures, ISS
cues --- see \texttt{CURSOR\_LOG.md} if they want proof.

\section{The one diagram to remember}
Memorize the three boxes: \textbf{Phone $\rightarrow$ API $\rightarrow$ World.}
If you only remember one picture from the deck, that is the one.
