\subsection{Sponsor Tracks}
\paragraph{Capital One Track: Best Use of Nessie.}
Capital One challenges you to reimagine the banking experience by building an
innovative application that leverages Capital One's Hackathon API, Nessie

including accounts, merchants, bills, and peer-to-peer transactions, Nessie is
your sandbox for solving real-world financial problems. We want to see projects
that creatively integrate Nessie's endpoints to empower users and improve their
financial lives.

Space gives you more to work with than almost any other field. You have access
to enormous public datasets, decades of missions, and a literature no human can

  \item Bonus points if you use Grok Bot for project planning and team collaboration.

\section{Submission Process and Judging}
\important{Sunday 10/4 8:30 AM --- all projects must be submitted, no exceptions.}

\noindent Devpost: \url{https://bigredhacks2026.devpost.com/}

\medskip
Review the Devpost before submitting and set aside at least 5 minutes. You need:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A GitHub or Google Drive link to your entire project.
  \item Your team's deck for presenting to judges, as a \textbf{Google Drive link to a PDF}.
        It can include: project name and team members, problem statement and why
        it matters, your solution and demo screenshots, tech stack / how you built
        it, and impact and future potential.
\end{enumerate}

Devpost will ask which tracks you are submitting to and for project details:
Inspiration, What it does, How we built it, Challenges we ran into,
Accomplishments that we're proud of, What we learned, and What's next.

At \textbf{8:45 AM} you will receive a team number; set up at that numbered
table for judging.

\subsection*{Preliminary Judging --- 9:00 AM, PSB Atrium}
Science-fair style, \textbf{4 minutes per team}: 2-minute presentation and
2-minute Q\&A. Your team will be visited by 2--4 judges at your table.

\subsection*{People's Choice}
All projects are live on Devpost for public voting after 8:30 AM. Every
participant may upvote their favorites; the project with the most upvotes wins.

\subsection*{Finalist Demos --- 11:30 AM, Baker 200 Auditorium}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The top 5 teams in the BigRed Track present again.
  \item Each finalist has 4 minutes to present/demo and 2 minutes for questions.
  \item If you aren't called to present again, \textbf{that does not mean you
        didn't win a track}. Side-track winners are decided in preliminary
        judging and announced at the Closing Ceremony.
\end{itemize}
